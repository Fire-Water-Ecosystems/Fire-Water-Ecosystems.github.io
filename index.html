Research Linking Fire, Water, Ecosystems, and Communities

Our mission is to advance the understanding and prediction of fire, water, and ecosystem dynamics through integrated observation, modeling, and data-driven research to support resilient landscapes and communities.

Current Focus

  • Fire ecology, prescribed burning, and fire management in forest and grassland ecosystems.
  • Wildfire risk evaluation and forecasting with NFDRS, machine learning, Behave, and FARSITE.
  • Ecosystem transformation and carbon-water processes under climate change, fire, and disturbance.
  • Satellite remote sensing, GIS, and large environmental datasets for natural resource applications.

2026 Research Highlights

01

Future Fire Weather

Quan Zhang separates the warming and drying that increase extreme fire-weather days from the wind effects that regulate burn probability.

 02

Operational Fire Forecasting

Jia Yang's WDAF framework uses spatial fire-spread simulations to quantify daily danger as simulated fire size and historical percentile.

 03

Prescribed-Fire Frequency

Alex Desjardins links time since burn, fuel recovery, and fire weather to the suppression challenge in productive tallgrass prairie.

 04

Hourly Fire Progression

Shanmin Fang uses GOES-16 observations to characterize fast fire growth and its environmental drivers across 294 large U.S. wildfires.

 05

Future Irrigation Demand

Haoxuan Yang projects increasing crop-water requirements and highlights regional and crop-specific planning priorities through 2100.

Research Workflow

From Integrated Observations to Fire-Water-Ecosystem Insight

The lab integrates satellite observations, climate and weather data, process-based ecosystem models, hydrological models, fire behavior models, and reproducible geospatial workflows to study coupled fire, water, and ecosystem dynamics.

  1. PrepareCollect satellite, climate, hydrologic, vegetation, fire, and natural resource datasets.
  2. ValidateModel ecosystem processes, fire behavior, and landscape change, then validate spatial products.
  3. PublishPublish peer-reviewed papers, datasets, maps, and decision-support resources.
